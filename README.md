### 1.	Executive Summary
This analysis examines employee survey responses across multiple departments to evaluate overall engagement levels, identify organizational strengths and weaknesses, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving employee satisfaction. The analysis revealed an overall average survey score of 2.98 out of 4, corresponding to a 75% Satisfaction Index, with 74.52% of responses classified as positive (Agree or Strongly Agree). While employees generally expressed positive opinions regarding role clarity and supervisor support, opportunities exist to strengthen employee relationships, recognition, and growth across some departments.
### 2.	Problem
Employee satisfaction directly influences productivity, retention, and organizational effectiveness. Management requires insights into employee perceptions to:
Measure overall employee engagement.
Identify high-performing and low-performing departments.
Understand which workplace factors most influence employee satisfaction.
Support evidence-based HR decision-making.

### 3.	Project Objectives
The objectives of this analysis were to:
- Assess overall employee satisfaction.
- Identify survey questions with the highest and lowest average scores.
- Compare employee engagement across departments.
- Examine response distribution across satisfaction levels.
- Recommend strategies for improving employee engagement.
#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Responses		        15,000
- Average Survey Score	  	2.98 / 4
- Positive Response Rate  	74.52%
- Satisfaction Index		    75%

### 4.	Dashboard Overview
Question 1 : Which survey questions did respondents agree with or disagree with most?
From the Average Survey Score by Question Visual, the question "I know what is expected of me at work” seems to receive the highest rating, which shows respondent tend to agree more with it. However, questions like “In the last seven days, I have received recognition or praise for doing good work” and “I have a best friend at work” are ranked lowest showing most disagree with these questions.
Question 2: Are there patterns in employee satisfaction across departments?
From the visuals on Average Survey Score by Department, The Family Justice Center recorded the highest average engagement, while the Sheriff Department had the lowest.

### 5. Key Findings
#### Overall Employee Engagement : 
Employee engagement is generally positive, with an overall satisfaction index of 75% and nearly three-quarters of survey responses classified as positive.

#### Highest Rated Engagement Factors: 
Employees expressed the highest satisfaction in:
Role Expectations
Supervisor Support
Opportunity to perform meaningful work.
These findings suggest employees generally understand their responsibilities and receive adequate support from immediate supervisors.

#### Areas Requiring Improvement: 
Comparatively lower scores were observed in:
Workplace friendships
Learning and Growth opportunities
Accountability
These areas represent opportunities for targeted organizational improvement.

#### Departmental Insights
Employee engagement varies across departments. Some departments consistently recorded above-average satisfaction scores, while others displayed comparatively lower engagement levels across multiple survey dimensions. The Department Satisfaction Heatmap highlights these variations and enables management to identify departments requiring focused intervention.
#### Response Distribution
Most survey responses fell within the Agree and Strongly Agree categories, indicating generally positive employee perceptions. However, the presence of Disagree and Strongly Disagree responses suggests there remain specific engagement challenges affecting a subset of employees.
### 6. Recommendations (Question 3: As an employer, what steps might you take to improve employee satisfaction based on the survey results?)
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

a.	Expand Employee Recognition Programs: Recognition-related survey items recorded comparatively lower scores. Management should implement formal employee recognition initiatives to acknowledge exceptional performance.

b.	Strengthen Career Development: Employees should be provided with additional Professional training, Mentorship programs, Career progression pathways to improve perceptions of learning and growth.

c. 	Improve Cross-Team Collaboration: Lower scores relating to workplace relationships suggest opportunities to strengthen collaboration through Team-building activities, Cross-functional projects and Employee engagement events.

d. 	Focus on Low-Performing Departments: Departments recording consistently lower engagement scores should receive targeted HR interventions, including Employee listening sessions, Leadership coaching and Department-specific engagement action plans

e. 	Continuous Monitoring: Employee engagement should be monitored through periodic surveys and dashboard updates to evaluate the effectiveness of improvement initiatives over time.

### 7. Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates that employee engagement across the organization is generally positive, with strong performance in role clarity and supervisor support. However, opportunities remain to strengthen employee recognition, professional development, and workplace relationships.
